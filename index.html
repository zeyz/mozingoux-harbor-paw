THE LITTLE DETAILS

Before the
first tail wag.

What do I bring?

A secure leash, current rabies documentation and any notes about your dog's health or behavior. Share sensitivities before the visit.

How long is an appointment?

Allow roughly two to three hours for a full groom. Coat condition, breaks and the chosen service affect timing.

What about nervous dogs?

Let us know ahead of time. A short introduction may be the best first step. If a dog is too stressed to continue, we pause and discuss options.

Do you groom cats?

This small concept studio is designed for dogs. Cat grooming would need a specialist with the right space and experience.
